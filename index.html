Reader tool concept · Harris County

Foreclosure Navigator

Texas runs one of the fastest foreclosure processes in the country — as few as 41 days from first notice to the auction steps. Houston metro logged 13,009 foreclosure starts in 2025, among the highest of any U.S. metro. This tool reads the statutory clock for your situation and shows what you can still do at every stage.

Which kind of foreclosure? The clocks are completely different.

The tax track is a different legal process — the taxing authority must sue and win a court judgment before any sale, and unlike the mortgage track, you CAN buy the home back afterward. If you are 65+ or disabled with a homestead, a tax-deferral affidavit (Tax Code §33.06) can postpone collection — the tax still accrues with interest, but the foreclosure stops while the deferral holds.

HOA foreclosure generally requires a court's approval for residential assessment liens, and many associations must offer a payment-plan option (Prop. Code §209.0062 — exceptions apply by association size and prior defaults). After an HOA sale there is a 180-day redemption right (§209.011) that runs from the association's post-sale notice to you, and the request must move by certified mail. If you received HOA foreclosure papers, the legal-aid card below is the first call — the timelines are strict and the mail rules matter.

Deadlines, stage guidance and legal-aid referrals are always free. No account required for the answer.

    Why the flagged row is the point: because every sentence must carry its basis, a simple comparison catches the model saying 10:00 when its own quoted source says 11:00 — and routes that row to a human instead of a reader. That is Meeting Monitor's discrepancy-checker pattern, demonstrated on a live error, on a real document, on the first try. A time-of-sale error in a foreclosure tool is exactly the class of mistake this architecture exists to stop.
